Frequently asked questions

Before you download the brochure.

What is in the project brochure?

The 3D renderings, the sales floor plan of every available apartment, the price list and the VAT recovery simulation.

The project is sold off-plan: what does that mean?

Construction has not started yet. Interior images are non-contractual 3D renderings. It is also when the choice is widest: four apartments are already sold and one is under offer.

How does VAT recovery work?

The apartment is run with hotel-style services: when you are away, a concierge rents it out. In return, you buy excluding VAT and recover the 20% VAT, with reduced notary fees. You keep your stays whenever you like.

I live outside France — is it complicated?

No. A chartered accountant specialising in non-residents recovers your VAT and files your French returns, the concierge handles the rentals, and an international agency guides you through the whole purchase, in your language.
